SpaceX Milestones

Key firsts on the road to reusable spaceflight — scroll for more

2002
SpaceX Is Founded

Elon Musk founds Space Exploration Technologies Corp. with the goal of making spaceflight affordable.

SEPTEMBER 2008
First Privately-Funded Rocket to Reach Orbit

Falcon 1 becomes the first privately developed, liquid-fueled rocket to reach Earth orbit.

DECEMBER 2010
First Commercial Spacecraft Recovered From Orbit

A Dragon capsule is launched, orbits Earth, and is recovered safely.

OCTOBER 2012
First Commercial Cargo Delivery to the ISS

Dragon docks with the International Space Station on the first operational CRS mission.

2013
Grasshopper Proves Vertical Landing Is Possible

The Grasshopper test vehicle completes a high-altitude vertical takeoff-and-landing flight.

DECEMBER 2015
First Landing of an Orbital-Class Rocket Booster

Falcon 9's first stage lands upright at Landing Zone 1 for the first time.

MARCH 2017
First Reflight of an Orbital-Class Booster

A previously flown Falcon 9 booster launches again on SES-10.

FEBRUARY 2018
Falcon Heavy's Maiden Flight

Falcon Heavy launches for the first time, with its two side boosters landing simultaneously.

MARCH 2019
Crew Dragon's First Trip to the ISS

Demo-1 sends an uncrewed Crew Dragon to dock with the station ahead of crewed flights.

MAY 2020
First Crewed Flight by a Private Company

Crew Dragon Demo-2 carries NASA astronauts to the ISS.

SEPTEMBER 2021
First All-Civilian Orbital Spaceflight

Inspiration4 sends a crew with no professional astronauts into orbit aboard Crew Dragon.

APRIL 2023
Starship's First Integrated Flight Test

The fully stacked Starship and Super Heavy booster fly together for the first time.

SEPTEMBER 2024
First Commercial Spacewalk

The Polaris Dawn crew performs the first spacewalk conducted by private citizens.

OCTOBER 2024
First Catch of a Super Heavy Booster by the Tower

Starship's Super Heavy booster is caught mid-air by "Mechazilla."
