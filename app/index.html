How to install

  1. On your Android phone, tap "Download for Android".
  2. When the download finishes, open the file (from the notification or the Downloads folder).
  3. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps ("Install unknown apps"). This is a standard Android prompt for any app that does not come from Google Play.
  4. Tap "Install". To update later, download and install the new version the same way - your favorites and reminders are kept.

Questions

Why isn't the app on Google Play?

Not yet. Until it is, it is offered here directly, as many independent apps are. It is the same app, signed by us, and it updates over the version you have.

Is it free?

Yes - free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Does it collect my data?

Your favorites, reminders and settings stay on your phone. The app shows the website, which counts anonymous visits with Google Analytics - see the privacy policy.
